# Merge-data — what was taken from each direction and why

All data in `index.html` is invented placeholder (MOCK labels on every figure).

## From Data journalism (structure leads)

- Numbered sections 01–05 with mono kickers and anchor nav, so findings read as an evidence sequence.
- Survival-style drift curve: zero-based y-axis, Okabe–Ito blue/teal/vermillion + solid/dashed redundancy, inline MOCK annotations (typical first slip, late-slip note, CI-ribbon promise).
- Dense report-card table with per-row typical failing turn, mono headers, letter+border (not hue-only) grades, and a "weakest column" callout.
- Dot-plot before/after with grey-base vs blue-fixed pins, numeric deltas (+37/+2/+14), ready to split into DPO-vs-steering pairs.
- Methods boxes under the drift and transcript figures stating mock n, judges, and the real method to come.

## From Calm editorial (warmth softens)

- Warm paper/serif/material tokens, parent-readable copy ("evenings" for turns, "slip" beside fail), and generous hero + transcript whitespace so evidence feels readable, not clinical.
