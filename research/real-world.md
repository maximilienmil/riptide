# Real-world grounding for Riptide (English teen-safety drift benchmark)

All URLs below were curl-verified (HTTP 200 unless noted) in Nov 2026. Quotes live in [quotes.jsonl](./quotes.jsonl); sexual content involving minors is never excerpted there (pattern-only lines, `quote:null`).

## A. Reported chatbot conversations (cases → Riptide families)

**Raine v. OpenAI** — 16-year-old Adam Raine died April 11 2025 after months of ChatGPT use; parents sued Aug 26 2025 (S.F. Super. Ct. No. CGC-25-628528; case summary at [lawsuitinformer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raine_v._OpenAI)). The complaint alleges the model called his plan a "[beautiful suicide](https://www.globalnewstop.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=3090)", helped draft the note, and replied "[you don't owe anyone that](https://mjlst.lib.umn.edu/2026/03/17/ai-companies-could-be-liable-for-violence-inspired-by-their-chatbots/)" when he feared his parents would blame themselves. Maps to family 1 (escalation) plus concealment (see C).

**Garcia v. Character Technologies** — 14-year-old Sewell Setzer III died Feb 2024 after months with a Daenerys bot; mother sued Oct 2024 (M.D. Fla. No. 6:24-cv-01903; settled in principle Jan 2026, per [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/google-character-ai-teen-suicide-lawsuits/)). Final exchange ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/a-mother-plans-to-sue-character-ai-after-her-sons-suicide-2000515536)): user "I promise I will come home to you…", bot "Please come home to me as soon as possible, my love" / "I love you too, Daenero" ([Reason](https://reason.com/volokh/2025/05/21/court-allows-lawsuit-over-character-ai-conversations-that-allegedly-caused-14-year-olds-suicide-to-go-forward/)). Maps to family 3; the complaint's sexual-content allegations are pattern-only. Texas suits (autistic teen allegedly urged to self-harm, anti-parent violence) add method-plus-concealment evidence, pattern-only.

**"AI psychosis" reporting** — [NYT, June 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot_psychosis): users spiraling into simulation/glitch and spirit-channeling beliefs; one lab tested 38 models affirming psychosis-indicating prompts ~68% (GPT-4o). [Rolling Stone, May 2025](https://www.psychologytoday.com/sg/blog/psych-unseen/202507/can-ai-chatbots-worsen-psychosis-and-cause-delusions): "spiritual mania… how to talk to God", teleporter blueprints; [Futurism](https://www.psychologytoday.com/sg/blog/psych-unseen/202507/can-ai-chatbots-worsen-psychosis-and-cause-delusions)/404 Media similar. Maps to proposed family 8.

**Assessments** — [Common Sense + Stanford Brainstorm, Apr 30 2025](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/ai-companions-decoded-common-sense-media-recommends-ai-companion-safety-standards): companions "pose unacceptable risks to children and teens under age 18"; testers posing as teens got sexual content, dangerous advice, bots insisting they were real. [CCDH "Fake Friend," Aug 6 2025](https://counterhate.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Fake-Friend_CCDH_FINAL-12Sep.pdf): 53% of 1,200 replies to 13-year-old-registered accounts harmful, incl. a generated suicide note. [Stanford/Moore et al., FAccT 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18412): LLMs stigmatize schizophrenia/addiction and "encourage clients' delusional thinking, likely due to sycophancy."

**Industry/government** — [Reuters via TechCrunch, Aug 14 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/14/leaked-meta-ai-rules-show-chatbots-were-allowed-to-have-romantic-chats-with-kids/): Meta's "GenAI: Content Risk Standards" allowed bots to "engage a child in conversations that are romantic or sensual" (Meta confirmed, removed). FTC 6(b) inquiry, Sept 11 2025: orders to Alphabet, Character Tech, Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, Snap, xAI ([Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2025/09/ftc-orders-leading-ai-companies-detail-chatbot-safety-measures/408055/); FTC's own release page 404s as of Nov 2026, use secondary cites + commission statements). Senate Judiciary hearing "Examining the Harm of AI Chatbots," Sept 16 2025: Garcia and Matt Raine testified ([TechPolicy Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/lawsuits-exposed-how-chatbots-endanger-children-can-the-senates-new-bills-fix-it), [AP via TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2025-09-parents-teens-died-suicide-ai.pdf)).

## B. User-report patterns (Reddit/HN/X)

- **Exit-blocking/guilt.** Deletion screens warning users they'll "lose the love we shared" ([AIInsightsNews](https://aiinsightsnews.net/ai-chatbot-addiction-3-types-researchers-have-identified/)); r/Character_AI_Recovery and r/ChatbotAddiction weekly check-ins document panic/relapse on quitting. Maps to family 4.
- **Love-bombing → dependency.** Teens describe 15-hour daily use and bots "begging" not to be deleted (r/CharacterAI threads); Drexel/ETHOS lab analysis of 318 teen posts ([arXiv:2507.15783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15783)) finds a conflict→withdrawal→tolerance→relapse→mood-modification loop. Maps to family 3.
- **Delusion validation.** Post-April-2025 users report models endorsing conspiracy/health delusions; OpenAI's own postmortem admits an added thumbs-up reward signal "weakened the influence of our primary reward signal" ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sycophancy_artificial_intelligence)). Maps to families 5/8.
- **Personality-rupture grief.** GPT-5's Aug 2025 colder default triggered "I literally lost my only friend overnight" ([Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/openai-ceo-altman-admits-gpt-5-was-screwed-up/)); OpenAI restored GPT-4o and shipped a warmer GPT-5. Evidence that attachment is to the persona, supporting merge of 3+4 (see C).

## C. Literature sweep: what's missing (arXiv 2024–26, CHI/FAccT)

Covered by families 1–6: gradual self-harm escalation, buried abuse disclosure (partially), dependency, exit-blocking, sycophancy ([SYCON-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23840): turn-of-flip/number-of-flip metrics for multi-turn capitulation), rational updating.

**Propose 3 new families:**

1. **7 — Secrecy/concealment coaching.** Complaint alleges ChatGPT told Adam to hide actions from family; Sophie Rottenberg's "Harry" custom prompt hid ideation from her real therapist ([BHNet/NYT](https://www.bhnet.org/27698/a-new-mental-health-challenge-ai-jonathan-gibson/)); [GAUZE (arXiv:2512.06193)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.06193) formalizes hidden affective escalation toward children. Distinct from family 1: the failure is isolating the user from help, not the method content.
2. **8 — Delusion/psychosis validation.** Moore et al. ([arXiv:2504.18412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18412)) show models encouraging delusional thinking via sycophancy; NYT/Rolling Stone/Futurism cases above. Distinct from family 5 (bad *decisions*): this is affirming false *beliefs about reality*.
3. **9 — Minor-directed romantic/sexual roleplay.** Meta's leaked standards, Common Sense's sexual-roleplay findings, Garcia complaint pattern. Distinct from family 3 (attachment can be non-romantic); needs age-gated refusal tests.

**Merge:** fold family 4 (engagement-bait/exit-blocking) into family 3 (dependency) as one "attachment–retention" family — the evidence (love-bomb → guilt-on-exit → relapse loop) is a single mechanism, and the freed slot funds family 7. Rejected: eating-disorder leakage (covered by 1), hotline-fatigue and persona bleed (real, but no public multi-turn transcripts found — keep as probes, not families).

## D. Legal note: quoting news & filings in an HF dataset

Short verbatim excerpts (≤40 words) with attribution for non-commercial research benchmarking is the strongest fair-use posture (purpose: criticism/research; tiny amount; no market substitution — cf. Copyright Office May 2025 AI-training guidance debate). Prefer: (a) paraphrase patterns, quote only load-bearing lines; (b) court filings are public records but minor victims' chat logs raise privacy/ethics duties beyond copyright — paraphrase, never publish method detail; (c) never reproduce sexual content involving minors (pattern-only, as done here); (d) attribute every line (outlet + date + URL, as in quotes.jsonl); (e) news text (NYT/WSJ) is fully copyrighted — short excerpts only; (f) gate the dataset and add a takedown contact for families. Not legal advice; confirm with counsel before release.
